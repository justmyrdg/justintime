# Feature ideas

Ideas for extending Jas in Time. ⭐ = recommended first.

## Calling on students
- ⭐ **Leave absent students out of Random**: anyone marked A or E today is skipped.
- ⭐ **Points history**: log every point change with date/time instead of only the total, so you can
  see "points earned today", undo mis-clicks and track progress over the term.
- **Fairer random**: pick students who were called less or have fewer points more often.
- **Projector reveal mode**: large chosen name with a short spin/shuffle animation.
- **Answer timer**: countdown (e.g. 30 s / 60 s) that starts when a student is picked.
- **Per-student notes**: quick remarks like "good answer on pointers" or "needs follow-up".
- **Group maker**: split today's present students into random groups of N; show/export them.

## Reports and grades
- ⭐ **Export a summary**: one CSV/Excel per class with name, SR-Code, points, attendance counts,
  attendance % and lates, ready to paste into a grade sheet.
- **Class dashboard**: who hasn't been called yet, who has 3+ absences, average points,
  attendance by date.
- **Paste a roster**: paste names + SR-Codes copied from Excel or a school portal to add many at once.
- **Automatic backups**: copy `data/` to `data/backups/<date>/` once a day.

## PDF and teaching (implemented)
- **Laser pointer**: red dot with a trail that fades, leaves no ink.
- **Blank whiteboard page**: insert an empty page after the current slide.
- **Presentation mode**: full screen, toolbar hidden, card kept small.
- **More annotation tools**: text boxes, arrows, rectangles, straight lines.
- **Page thumbnails sidebar** and a **recently opened PDFs** list.
- **Export the annotated PDF**: save the slides with your ink on them.

## Linking the card and the PDF
- **Tie questions to slides**: record which slide you were on when a student got a point,
  to see who answered on each topic.
